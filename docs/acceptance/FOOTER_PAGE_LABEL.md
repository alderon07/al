# Name the footer page

## Acceptance criteria

- The F3 page is called `Footer` in the TUI navigation, help, and user documentation.
- Its heading remains `Compose your footer`; the fields and keyboard shortcuts keep their current behavior.
- Internal configuration settings and unrelated uses of the word `settings` keep their meaning.
- The compiled TUI shows `Footer` in a real terminal at narrow and wide widths, and `make fmt check` passes.
